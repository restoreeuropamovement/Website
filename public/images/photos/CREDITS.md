# Photograph sources and licences

Every photograph on this site is recorded here: where it came from, who took it,
and under what licence. Kept so that a licensing question years from now can be
answered from the repository rather than from memory.

All files are stored and served from this origin. Nothing is hotlinked, which is
what allows `/privacy` to state that loading a page discloses your visit to no
third party.

## Licence

All three photographs below are used under the [Unsplash
License](https://unsplash.com/license), which grants:

> an irrevocable, nonexclusive, worldwide copyright license to download, copy,
> modify, distribute, perform, and use images from Unsplash for free, including
> for commercial purposes, without permission from or attributing the
> photographer or Unsplash

Attribution is therefore **not required**. It is recorded here anyway, because
knowing the provenance of an image is worth more than the few lines it costs.

The licence excludes one thing that does not apply here: compiling Unsplash
images to build a competing stock-photo service. It also restricts use for
machine-learning and facial-identification purposes, which likewise does not
apply.

Note that images served from `plus.unsplash.com` are **Unsplash+**, a paid tier
under a different licence. None are used here, and none should be added without
buying the subscription.

## Two standing rules

**No recognisable faces.** On a political website an identifiable person reads
as an endorsement they did not give, and no licence can grant permission the
subject never offered. Landscapes, architecture, and distant or obscured figures
only.

**Europe only.** Stock searches for things like "terraced fields" return
mostly Asian rice terraces, which are often the better photographs — and would
be quietly false on a page arguing for European continuity. Every image above
was checked for where it was taken, not just what it looks like.
